# Visual assets

The two root PNG files are the user-supplied visual references and are preserved intact. `public/images/masthead.webp` is a web-optimized copy of the supplied masthead; the other WebP files are cropped sample photo regions from the supplied homepage reference. They are explicitly demonstration artwork, not evidence or verified journalistic photographs. Replace them with licensed original images for public launch.

Bengali fonts are self-hosted Noto Serif Bengali and Noto Sans Bengali, bundled through Fontsource. Their SIL Open Font License texts are included in `public/fonts/licenses/` and must remain in a distribution that includes these fonts.
